# No time estimates for engineering work

I don't want estimates in engineer-days, hours, or weeks for implementation work, in any write-up, reply, or brief.

- **When I ask "how much work is this", describe the scope instead:** the work items, what's new to the codebase, and the risks or unknowns that could make it grow.
- **Applies everywhere:** design docs, option comparisons, architecture-style outputs, and briefs to subagents. Tell subagents not to produce estimates either.
